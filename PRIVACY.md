# Cue privacy policy

Cue does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- Cue for Mac and Cue for iPhone and iPad talk to each other directly over your local network, encrypted. Nothing passes through a server operated by us.
- Your deck (pages, pads, settings) is stored on your Mac and cached on your device. It never leaves them.
- Cue reads what it needs to show honest pad state (which app is in front, whether the microphone is muted, what is playing) on the Mac only, and shows it on your device. It does not record or keep this information.
- No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no accounts.

If you use the Terminal, shell or AppleScript pads, they run what you typed, on your Mac, when you press them. Cue does not send them anywhere.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyimjames/cue-releases/issues
